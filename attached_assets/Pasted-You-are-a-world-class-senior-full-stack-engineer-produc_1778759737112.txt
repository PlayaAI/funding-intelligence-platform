## PRODUCT CONTEXT

This product was inspired by a grant strategy meeting where the team reviewed Instrumentl and decided to build their own internal grant intelligence platform.

The product has two connected layers:

### 1. Public Proof Website

A public-facing website that proves the organization/community is credible and grant-ready.

It should show:
- Who the organization/community is
- What projects have been built
- What workshops happened
- What tools and experiments exist
- What impact/proof already exists
- Why funders should trust the team
- Which initiatives are grant-ready

The public site is proof-driven, not AI-hype-driven.

### 2. Internal Grant Intelligence Dashboard

A private dashboard that helps the team:
- Add projects
- Add grant opportunities
- Match projects to grants
- Track deadlines
- Research funders
- Research peer organizations
- Manage applications
- Link Google Docs / Google Drive folders
- Assign tasks
- Build reusable proof packages
- Prioritize the top 3 grants
- Avoid last-minute grant chaos
- Use AI later for summaries, fit scoring, drafting, and proof suggestions
