## Projects

### Connect App
- Category: Human connection / social technology.
- Summary: A guided interaction tool that helps people connect more deeply with someone they are meeting or already know.
- Problem: Many social interactions remain shallow, fragmented, and device-mediated.
- Solution: A guided experience that prompts deeper questions and eventually asks users to put the phone down and look into each other’s eyes.
- Audience: Intentional communities, event participants, workshop attendees, people seeking deeper connection.
- Stage: MVP / field testing.
- Grant relevance: social cohesion, loneliness reduction, community building, humane technology.

### Burning Man Packing List

### Biohack Your Burn
